Learn at your own pace

Useful information for informed buyers.

Start with the topics that matter most to your purchase. These guides are educational starting points—not individualized financial or legal advice.

01 / Financing

Preapproval basics

Understand what a lender may review, what a preapproval letter represents, and why your budget should account for more than the purchase price.

02 / Budgeting

Upfront and ongoing costs

Learn about down payment, closing costs, inspections, insurance, property taxes, utilities, maintenance, and reserves.

03 / Property search

Build a realistic wish list

Separate must-haves from preferences. Consider commute, property condition, HOA rules, layout, and future needs.

04 / Due diligence

Inspections and disclosures

Understand why reviewing disclosures and arranging appropriate inspections can be important parts of evaluating a property.

05 / Offers

Understanding offer terms

Price is only one part of an offer. Financing, contingencies, deposits, timing, and other terms can matter.

06 / California programs

Researching assistance programs

Program availability, eligibility, funding, and requirements change. Verify current details directly with the administering agency or an approved lender.

For current California assistance program information, confirm eligibility and availability with the official program administrator. Do not rely on outdated social posts or assume funds are available.
