Know what comes next

A clearer path to homeownership.

Every purchase is different, but understanding the common stages can help you prepare better questions and make informed decisions.

01

Define your goals

Identify preferred areas, property type, must-haves, flexibility, and your ideal timeline.

02

Speak with a lender

Discuss your financial picture, loan options, estimated payment, cash needed, and preapproval requirements directly with a qualified mortgage professional.

03

Build your search

Use your criteria to focus the search. Confirm property details, availability, and showing arrangements before making plans.

04

Evaluate and offer

Review comparable information, disclosures, property condition, offer terms, contingencies, and deadlines with appropriate professionals.

05

Escrow and due diligence

Track contract dates, inspections, appraisal, title, insurance, lender conditions, and document review.

06

Final review and closing

Complete lender and escrow requirements, review final documents, conduct the final walkthrough, and confirm closing arrangements.

This is a general educational overview, not legal, tax, lending, or financial advice. Contract terms and timelines vary. Your lender, escrow/title professionals, inspectors, and other qualified advisors can explain matters within their respective roles.
